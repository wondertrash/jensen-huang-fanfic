## Part I: The Fall

Hensen Juang: rich billionaire, owner of NVIDIA, CEO.

Then the scandals start:

1. His **1st affair** gets out, and he admits it. It may have been with a Chinese spy.
2. A **2nd affair** gets out (with an NVIDIA employee).
3. A **3rd affair** gets out (with another NVIDIA employee).
4. He **resigns / gets fired**.
5. He's **divorced**, and his wife gets half (**$80B**).
6. A **4th affair** gets out (with an NVIDIA employee).
7. A **5th affair** gets out (with an AMD exec).

The AMD CEO, his cousin **Sisa Lu**, laughs and posts on the AMD account:

> **ceo of the year: amd ceo**

Juang sees this and is **enraged**.

---

## Part II: The Method

His ex-wife kicks him out, and he walks down the street. He decides he needs to **LOCK IN** and beat Lisa, but he doesn't know how.

Then, on the side of the road, he sees a beggar.

The beggar is making a deal with some sort of dealer, but Juang can't tell what. He walks closer, and on a closer look he sees they're dealing some weird-smelling plants.

That's when an idea strikes.

Juang **MUST** start working with those plants. The dealer is making money from *beggars*, and back at NVIDIA, Juang couldn't even begin to get beggars to buy his chips.

He realizes: ***THAT'S THE METHOD.***

---

## Part III: The Warehouse

He follows the dealer to a warehouse.

Inside are rows and rows of shelves full of plants. One row sticks out to Juang, because it also holds this **weird glowing liquid**. Curious, he tries to climb the shelf to get a closer look.

That was his mistake.

As soon as his hand touches the glass, the shelf falls backwards, taking Juang with it. All the plants and jars crash on the floor and mix together, all over Juang.

Juang is knocked out cold by the fall.

---

## Part IV: The Awakening

*A few hours later...*

Juang wakes up in the hospital. A nurse is beside him.

> **Nurse Bu:** He's awake!
>
> **Juang:** Hey there, are you busy tonight?
>
> **Nurse Bu:** I'm not interested bro. U just got caught for FIVE affairs. I'm not going to date a player.
>
> **Juang:** Think about it baby.
>
> **Nurse Bu:** I'm going to report you for harassment if you don't stop. BUTTT, I'll be fine with it if you give me some NVIDIA stocks ;)
>
> **Juang:** Yeah sure, whatever you want baby.
>
> **Juang:** But wait, it's soo dark in here.
>
> **Nurse Bu:** Wdym?
>
> **Juang:** I want it to be BRIIIIIIIIGHT.
>
> **Nurse Bu:** Wh-

All of a sudden, **red beams of light shoot out of Juang's eyes.**

> **Juang:** I CAN'T STOP IT!

His laser eyes go straight through the roof, the TV, and everything else he looks at.

> **Nurse Bu:** CLOSE YOUR EYES!

He tries.

> **Juang:** OWWWWWWWWWWW!

He burnt his eyelids.

Then he blinks, and everything seems fine.

> **Nurse Bu:** Bro what was that.
>
> **Juang:** Idk yo.
>
> **Nurse Bu:** Ok, now that you got that sorted, what are u gunna do with your newfound powers? Are you going to save the world?
>
> **Juang:** I have a lot of ideas of what I'm going to do with my powers, but what do you mean *save*?
>
> **Nurse Bu:** I mean that now that you're a superhero, you should go out and save the world and fight crime.
>
> **Juang:** Save? The world doesn't need saving. **It needs fixing.**

🎵 ***\*theme song\**** 🎵

> **Nurse Bu:** ...Fixing? And who would be in charge of fixing it?
>
> **Juang:** Me. It's up to me to fix the world. And the first thing I'm going to fix is **AMD**.
>
> **Nurse Bu:** What are you going to do to AMD?
>
> **Juang:** I'm going to destroy the CEO.

## Part V: The Ride

Juang blasts out the window with his newfound powers and jumps. He lands in a private car with his chauffeur.

> **Juang:** We're going to Sisa Lu's house. Pronto.
>
> **Driver Drump:** Do you got payment for this ride?
>
> **Juang:** Yeah. *(his eyes glowing red)*

## Part VI: The Reveal

*30 minutes later...*

They arrive at Sisa Lu's house. The lights aren't on.

Juang blows off the door, careful not to destroy anything else. He wants to *see* it when he blows her head off.

He walks in and checks each bedroom, eyes glowing, ready to shoot.

All of a sudden he hears footsteps coming from the back of the house. He recognizes the outfit.

It's the hot **Nurse Bu**.

*"What's she doing here?"* he thinks, confused about why she would follow him.

Somehow she doesn't notice the mess Juang made. She just goes to her bedroom and changes out of her work clothes, into **coral-coloured business attire**.

It clicks.

> **Juang:** ***NURSE BU IS JUST SISA LU IN DISGUISE. AND SHE'S NOT REALLY A NURSE.***

The woman he thought was so attractive, the one he was obsessed with, was someone he knew well. She was the girl he grew up with, had Thanksgiving with, had Christmases with.

**She was his cousin.**

But for some reason, he liked it. It made him more attracted. He was obsessed.

He jumps down from his perch and enters her bedroom.

> **Juang:** SISA, IT WAS YOU. IT WAS ALWAYS YOU.

She looks at Juang, confused and scared.
